# Group Home

A workforce and operations platform for Adult Foster Care (AFC) operators: scheduling, time clock and timesheets first, then resident care.

## Language

**Home**:
One licensed AFC house run by an operator (e.g. Merritt, Dixie, Southfield). Each has its own time zone.
_Avoid_: House, location, facility, site

**Staff**:
A person who works shifts at one or more Homes. Also the name of the non-admin role.
_Avoid_: Caregiver, DCW, employee, worker
